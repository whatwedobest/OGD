When MCA payments become the problem

Several daily or weekly withdrawals can leave even a profitable business short on operating cash. Payroll, vendors, inventory and rent all compete with existing positions.

Another advance usually adds pressure instead of relieving it. Gateway starts with the full picture: every funder, every balance and every payment, measured against what the business brings in.

What a Gateway review covers

A Gateway review maps every outstanding MCA position, checks it against the business's cash flow, and explains the options that fit, including what each one would mean for payments, timing and cost.

How a Gateway review works

  1. 01Share the detailsThe merchant or broker sends the business and MCA position details.
  2. 02ReviewWe go through the statements, positions and cash flow.
  3. 03Options explainedThe merchant sees the options, with payments, timing and costs.
  4. 04Merchant decidesIf the merchant chooses an option, the plan starts on agreed terms.

Who may qualify?

There are no hard rules, and exceptions are made case by case. As a guide:

  • An operating business with verifiable revenue
  • Multiple outstanding MCA positions
  • $30,000 or more in total MCA debt
  • $8,000 or more in combined monthly obligations
  • Recent bank statements and position details available

Gateway FAQ

Is Gateway Consolidators a lender?
No. Gateway Consolidators is a service brand of OpenGate Data. It does not make loans or advances. It reviews a business's existing MCA obligations and explains the options available.
Will my payments go down?
It depends on the business and the option chosen, and no reduction is guaranteed. Every option is explained with its payments, timing and total cost so the owner can decide.
Can a business that was just declined for funding be reviewed?
Yes. A recent decline doesn't prevent a Gateway review.
Do you work with businesses nationwide?
We work with more than 300 referral partners across the country. Whether a particular option is available depends on the business's situation and location, which we confirm during the review.
Will the referring broker stay involved?
If they want to. Some brokers stay anonymous, and others take part in every step.
