OpenGate gives brokers one clear path for hard-to-place files, from the first call to a commission on files that convert.

Submit a Deal Review Criteria

Brokers connect with OpenGate, submit an authorized file through a secure upload, and within hours the file is on their own CRM board with an underwriter ready to call. Qualified files move through underwriting, and the broker is paid on files that convert under the partner agreement.

  1. 01ConnectTalk with our team about your business, the programs available, referral terms and how you want merchants contacted.
  2. 02SubmitSend the file and the merchant's bank statements through the secure upload, with their authorization.
  3. 03Review, within hoursWe collect the data, add it to your CRM board and have an underwriter ready to call.
  4. 04AdvanceQualified files move through underwriting. The assigned underwriter keeps your board current.
  5. 05Get paidYou receive the outcome and your commission on files that convert, under your agreement.
