Does your file qualify?

There are no hard rules. We want every opportunity we can work. As a guide, files that fit best have:

  • $30,000 or more in total MCA debt
  • $8,000 or more in combined monthly MCA obligations
  • Recent business bank statements and merchant contact details
  • The merchant's authorization to share their information

Files below these levels are considered case by case.
