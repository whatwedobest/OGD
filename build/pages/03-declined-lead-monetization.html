Declined leads FAQ

What is declined lead monetization?
It's sending previously declined business funding prospects for a second review against other solutions, so the files can still generate referral revenue.
Do I need to buy new leads?
No. The program works with leads you already have, as long as you're authorized to share the merchant's information.
Is every declined lead eligible?
No. Each file is reviewed on its own. Files with $30,000+ in MCA debt and $8,000+ in combined monthly obligations fit best, and others are considered case by case.
How soon can I submit after a decline?
The same day, as long as you have the merchant's authorization to share their information.
