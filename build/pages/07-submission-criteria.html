A better submission starts with the right information.

Accurate business details and the right documents get you the fastest answer.

There are no hard rules. Files below these levels are considered case by case.

Initial information

The details

  • Merchant's legal business name, state and authorized contact
  • Your name, company and contact details
  • Why the file was declined, if you know
  • Current MCA positions, payment frequency and approximate amounts
  • How you'd like us to contact the merchant
Documents

The paperwork

  • Recent business bank statements, as PDFs
  • Payoff letters or balance details, if you have them
  • The merchant's authorization to share their information

Statements go through a secure upload and are only accessible to authorized OpenGate and Gateway personnel reviewing the file. The underwriter will confirm anything else needed.

What happens next

Within hours, your file is on your CRM board with an underwriter ready to call. The underwriter may ask for more documents as the file moves forward.

A submission is not an approval, and it does not guarantee a commission.
