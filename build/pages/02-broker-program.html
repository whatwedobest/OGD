Broker program FAQ

Is there a minimum number of submissions?
No minimum. Submit one file or a full book.
Can I submit a file another lender declined today?
Yes. Eligible files can be submitted the same day they're declined, as long as you have the merchant's authorization to share their information.
How is my merchant's data handled?
Statements are submitted through a secure upload and are only accessible to authorized OpenGate and Gateway personnel involved in reviewing the file.
How fast will I hear back?
Within hours of submitting a file, we collect the data, add it to your CRM board and have an underwriter ready to call, unless you ask us to handle it differently.
Who contacts the merchant?
It depends on you. Some partners stay anonymous and some want to be involved at every step. Ideally, you set up a discovery call or let the merchant know we'll be in touch.
How are commissions paid?
Commissions are paid on files that convert, under the terms of your signed partner agreement. We'll walk you through the current structure on a call.
