What we offer

OpenGate Records provides three kinds of business funding data: public UCC filings, MCA submission data and opt-in responder lists. Each record set is delivered in the format your team uses, and pricing is set case by case.

UCC filings

Recorded funding activity

Businesses with UCC filings that point to prior or current financing.

MCA submissions

Recent applicants

Merchants who recently applied for business funding through partner channels.

Responder lists

Opted-in owners

Business owners who asked to hear about financing through forms and campaigns.
